# Plume: regulatory milestone

**TL;DR:** The November 10, 2026 announcement describes a real regulatory achievement. It strengthens Plume's business infrastructure, but does not establish that its planned products are live or that PLUME will appreciate.

Kimber Digital Securities, Plume's affiliated broker-dealer, has FINRA membership and SEC broker-dealer registration. [FINRA BrokerCheck](https://brokercheck.finra.org/firm/summary/339774) independently lists an active firm: **CRD 339774**, **SEC 8-71468**, last approval **October 27, 2026**. November 10 is the announcement date. [Verification snapshot](registration-check.json).

The [company announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kimber-digital-securities-becomes-a-registered-broker-dealer-bringing-real-us-securities-onchain-302935166.html) outlines delivery of securities entitlements to wallets, complementing its existing transfer-agent infrastructure. This is meaningful progress toward regulated tokenization, rather than evidence of a completed operating stack.

Two limits matter: DTC-related offerings remain under development, and a securities entitlement through intermediaries is different from direct registration on an issuer's shareholder register. Registration is not SEC/FINRA endorsement of Plume, its products, or the PLUME token.

**Assessment:** A credible milestone with commercial potential. Execution, customer adoption and token economics determine whether it becomes a successful investment. See the [next-months outlook and Ondo comparison](OUTLOOK.md).
